## What we changed

- Cut a loop of 32 bars (69.818 s) from 8.194 s into the source, with a 26182 sample crossfade at the wrap.
- Resampled to 48 kHz, set the loudness to about -16 LUFS (-0.20 dB), and encoded to Opus (Ogg) and AAC (MP4) at 96 kb/s.
