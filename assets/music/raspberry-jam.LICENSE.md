## What CC0 allows

The author gave up all copyright and related rights in the work (CC0 1.0). Skate ATL may copy, change, and publish the track, also for commercial use, with no attribution. We credit the author all the same, in this file and in `cc0-sources.json`.

## What we changed

- Cut a loop of 32 bars (68.417 s) from 52.414 s into the source, with a 25656 sample crossfade at the wrap.
- Resampled to 48 kHz, set the loudness to about -16 LUFS (-3.30 dB), and encoded to Opus (Ogg) and AAC (MP4) at 96 kb/s.
