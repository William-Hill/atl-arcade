## 3. Sensitive topics and neutral angles

- **Freedom Parkway:** A planned freeway would have split Inman Park and Poncey-Highland. Use "Neighbors worked together to shape Freedom Park." Do not mention the road plan, lost homes, or politics.
- **BeltLine and Historic Fourth Ward Park:** Critics link the parks to rising rents. Use only "opened 2011, first part of the BeltLine greenway, holds storm water."
- **Inman Park in the mid-1900s:** Use "restored by neighbors from 1969, National Register 1973."
- **Krog Street Tunnel:** It has a graffiti history and a 2014 whitewash dispute. Use "long-running outdoor mural gallery."
- **Stove Works:** Use "opened 1889". Do not mention the closing.
- **Civil War markers:** Skip them.
- **Ponce de Leon Springs and the amusement park before Sears:** Skip until a primary source is found.
