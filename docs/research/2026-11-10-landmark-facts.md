### Ford Factory Lofts

- **The Ford Motor Company Assembly Plant was built in 1914-15, four stories of reinforced concrete.**
  - Source: National Park Service, National Register of Historic Places, https://npgallery.nps.gov/GetAsset/89e18d7b-3b58-4434-9d83-bce98f805556
  - Quote: "The Ford Motor Company Assembly Plant, built in 1914-15, is a four-story, approximately rectangular industrial building with a partial basement. It is constructed of reinforced concrete."
- **Model T's (1915-1927), Model A's (1927-1932) and V-8's (1932-1937) were assembled here.**
  - Source: National Park Service, National Register of Historic Places, https://npgallery.nps.gov/GetAsset/89e18d7b-3b58-4434-9d83-bce98f805556
  - Quote: "At the Ponce de Leon Ford plant, Model T's (1915-1927), Model A's (1927-1932) and V-8's (1932-1937) were assembled."
- **The building was headquarters of Ford's Southeastern operations from 1915 to 1942.**
  - Source: National Park Service, National Register of Historic Places, https://npgallery.nps.gov/GetAsset/89e18d7b-3b58-4434-9d83-bce98f805556
  - Quote: "It served as the headquarters of Ford Motor Company's Southeastern operations from 1915 to 1942 and during that time was a major commercial presence in Atlanta."
- **A reinforced second floor carried a railbed, so rail cars rolled into the building to unload.**
  - Source: National Park Service, National Register of Historic Places, https://npgallery.nps.gov/GetAsset/89e18d7b-3b58-4434-9d83-bce98f805556
  - Quote: "A heavily reinforced floor along the central portion of the second level supported a railbed and allowed rail cars to be brought into the building and directly under the craneway for unloading."
- **It is one of three surviving early Ford assembly plants in the South, with Houston and Dallas.**
  - Source: National Park Service, National Register of Historic Places, https://npgallery.nps.gov/GetAsset/89e18d7b-3b58-4434-9d83-bce98f805556
  - Quote: "It is one of three extant Ford assembly plants in the South from this early period of expansion. (The others are located in Houston and Dallas.)"
