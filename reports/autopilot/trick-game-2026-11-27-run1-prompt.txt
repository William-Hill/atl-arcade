Play Skate ATL like a new player for 10 minutes, to test the trick input. The keys: W or Shift pushes, A and D steer, S brakes, Space jumps, F grinds on a rail or ledge (hold F off a rail for a manual), Q is TRICK, E is SPIN. A direction key (W, A, S, or D) held just before Q, E or F, or pressed at the same time, picks a different trick: on the ground Q starts a dance, in the air Q starts a grab, on a rail the direction picks the grind stance. A dance runs to its end; a press during a dance does nothing. Before EVERY press of Q, E, or F, call `game.call("atl.playtest", "intend", <dir>)` with the direction you want: n (no direction), l, r, u, or d. Mix all 5 directions, on the ground (dances), in the air (off a ramp or a jump), and on rails and ledges (the skatepark has rails, ledges and ramps). Skate as a player does: push with W and steer with A and D between tricks, and keep your speed up; a stopped skater does no trick. Also try to bank a combo of 3 links or more with a manual (for example a grind, a manual, then a grab). At the end, call `game.call("atl.playtest", "metrics")` and report the result, and every trial where the result was not the intended direction.
