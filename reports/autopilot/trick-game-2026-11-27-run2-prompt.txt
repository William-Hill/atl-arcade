Play Skate ATL for 10 minutes to test the trick input on rails and ledges. The keys: W or Shift pushes, A and D steer, S brakes, Space jumps, F grinds (hold F; off a rail, F gives a manual), Q is TRICK, E is SPIN. A direction key (W, A, S, or D) held just before F, or pressed at the same time, picks the grind stance on a rail: n, l, r, u, or d. Before EVERY press of Q, E, or F in play, call `game.call("atl.playtest", "intend", <dir>)` with the direction you want; do not call it in a research probe. Spend most of the time on grinds: find the rails and ledges of the skatepark, line up straight with the rail from 15 m or more, push to a high speed, jump just before the rail, and hold F with the direction key. Mix all 5 stance directions on the rails. Also bank combos of 3 links or more with a manual: grind, land, hold F for a manual, then jump and grab with Q. Keep your speed up; a stopped skater does no trick. At the end, call `game.call("atl.playtest", "metrics")` and report the result, and every trial where the result was not the intended direction.
